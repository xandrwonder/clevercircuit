Privacy Policy

This policy covers Wassup English for iPhone, iPad and Mac. The app runs without an account and does not send study activity to the developer. It uses no ad networks, trackers or third-party analytics.

Data on your device

Study sets, progress, practice and review history, favorites, personal vocabulary and preferences are stored on your device. The app uses this information to resume practice and organize review.

Microphone and speech recognition

Speaking exercises request microphone and speech recognition permissions. English speech recognition runs on the device when it is supported. The app uses the recognized text to check your answer without saving the text or audio to a file. You can skip the exercise or revoke its permissions in system settings.

Voices and reminders

Wassup English plays words and examples using Apple system voices. If you enable a reminder, the app schedules a local notification. You can disable the reminder in the app or in system settings.

Backups and erasing data

You can save a backup file and use it to restore your app data. You choose where to store the file and who can access it. Saving it to a cloud folder or sharing it makes it subject to the policies of the service you choose.

On iPhone and iPad, use Delete App to remove the app's local data. On Mac, some local data may remain after removing the app. Contact support for instructions. Delete separately saved backups wherever you stored them.

Support requests

If you contact support, we use your email address and the information you share to respond and investigate the issue. You can request deletion of your support correspondence by contacting support.

This website

GitHub Pages hosts this website. When you visit it, GitHub may process technical data to operate and secure the service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
