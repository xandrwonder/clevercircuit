Privacy Policy

The app is completely free, with no hidden purchases or subscriptions.

This policy covers Wassup English for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Aleksandr Koroviakovskii provides Wassup English for free and without an account. Usage analytics is off by default. Adults aged 18 or over can enable it for their own use. All features remain available if you decline.

Data on your device

Study sets, progress, practice and review history, favorites, personal vocabulary and preferences are stored on your device. The app uses this information to resume practice and organize review.

Microphone and speech recognition

Speaking exercises request microphone and speech recognition permissions. English speech recognition runs on the device when it is supported. The app uses the recognized text to check your answer without saving the text or audio to a file. You can skip the exercise or revoke its permissions in system settings.

Voices and reminders

Wassup English plays words and examples using Apple system voices. If you enable a reminder, the app schedules a local notification. You can disable the reminder in the app or in system settings.

Backups and erasing data

You can save a backup file and use it to restore your app data. You choose where to store the file and who can access it. Saving it to a cloud folder or sharing it makes it subject to the policies of the service you choose.

On iPhone and iPad, use Delete App to remove the app's local data. On Mac, some local data may remain after removing the app. Contact support for instructions. Delete separately saved backups wherever you stored them.

Optional usage analytics

With your consent, we use first-party analytics to understand which practice modes are used and improve the app. We receive random installation and local user identifiers, consent time, event types and timestamps, session identifiers, sections opened, types of exercises started, time in the app and counts of answers and correct answers. Session events include the app language, app build and operating system version.

These identifiers link events within Wassup English. The app does not transmit vocabulary, examples, answer text or audio, personal dictionary entries, names, email addresses, precise location or advertising identifiers. We do not share analytics with ad networks or link it to activity in other apps.

Server and retention

Analytics is sent over HTTPS to the developer's server in Türkiye. The server processes the network address to respond and limit load. It does not store IP addresses or browser user-agent strings in its analytics database. Detailed events and inactive identifiers are automatically deleted after 90 days. Pending events on the device are eligible for sending for up to seven days.

We may keep de-identified usage and practice summaries for groups of sufficient size without a time limit. These summaries contain no installation identifiers, individual events or individual user histories. We use them to understand long-term use of the app.

Withdrawing consent and deleting data

Open Practice Sessions → Settings → Privacy and turn off sharing of practice statistics. Collection stops at once. The app discards pending events and requests that linked data be deleted from the server. If you are offline, the request waits for a connection. Previously de-identified summaries remain. On-device practice history and progress stay available.

Support requests

If you contact support, we use your email address and the information you share to respond and investigate the issue. You can request deletion of your support correspondence by contacting support.
