Frequently asked questions

How do I move my progress to another device?

Open the settings in Practice Sessions and save a backup. Move the file to the other device, then choose to restore from a backup in its Wassup English settings. Restoring replaces the app's current data on that device.

Why can't I hear a word?

Check the media volume and audio output. Try another installed English voice in the app settings and check the automatic playback setting. Tap the speaker on a card to play the word again.

Why can't I use a speaking exercise?

Check microphone and speech recognition permissions in system settings. Speaking exercises require on-device English speech recognition. You can skip the exercise when this feature is unavailable.

Why didn't I receive my reminder?

Check the reminder time in the app, notification permissions and your current Focus settings.
