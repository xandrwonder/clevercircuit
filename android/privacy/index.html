MoreBrain for Android — Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026. Applies to MoreBrain for Android 1.0.0, package com.morebrain.android, published by Proterum Engineering.

MoreBrain for Android is provided by Aleksandr Koroviakovskii, Individual Entrepreneur, Georgia. It is an offline educational app. No account, advertising, subscription or in-app purchase is required.

Information on your device

The app stores local profile names and avatars, language and appearance preferences, workout settings, answers, training history, results, lesson progress and saved game state on your device. You choose what to enter. This information is used only to provide the app’s features.

No collection or sharing from the app

This Android version has no Internet permission and sends none of your app data to the developer or to third parties. It contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, account or payment SDK. We do not receive profile names, answers, results, device identifiers, contact details, location or usage events from the app. The optional analytics described in the separate iOS policy is not part of this Android version.

Storage and deletion

Local data remains on the device until you delete a local profile, clear the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Deleting a local profile removes that profile’s history and settings. Updates using the same package and signing identity preserve local data. This version disables Android automatic app backup; it does not provide cloud synchronization or a developer-managed backup service.

Children and families

The educational features may be used by families and children without an online account. This Android version does not transmit children’s profile information or activity, show ads, provide public profiles, messaging or social sharing.

Support and this website

If you email sales@qiroh.com, we receive your email address and whatever information you choose to include. We use that information to answer the request and retain it only as necessary for support and applicable legal obligations. Do not send passwords, verification codes or unnecessary personal information.

This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. Opening this website in a browser is separate from using the offline app. GitHub may process technical request information, such as your IP address and browser data, for security and site operation under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Changes and contact

If the Android app’s data practices change, we will update this policy and the relevant Google Play declarations before the change takes effect. Privacy questions or requests may be sent to sales@qiroh.com.
