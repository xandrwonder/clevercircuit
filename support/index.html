Support

Help with MoreBrain

Need help? Email sales@qiroh.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, App version, and a short description of the issue. Never send passwords, payment-card details, or Apple ID verification codes.

Use the fully free version

Update to version 2.3 or later from the App Store. All games, trainers, lessons, practice, settings and local profiles are available without subscriptions or in-app purchases. There is no purchase or Restore Purchases button in the free version. Update without deleting the App to keep local progress and settings.

Earlier Apple subscriptions

If you subscribed in an earlier version, on iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions and select MoreBrain. Check the status shown by Apple and cancel any subscription that still shows a future renewal. Deleting the App does not cancel a subscription.

Earlier payments and refunds

Apple processes App Store payments and refund requests. You can check eligibility at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot promise or issue an Apple refund on your behalf.

Optional analytics and deletion

Version 2.3 (build 14) introduces optional first-party analytics, off by default. Open Profile > Product analytics to review the collected fields or change your choice. Only an adult using their own profile should enable it; children and shared profiles can use every feature without analytics.

Turn off and delete linked data stops collection immediately and requests deletion from the server. If offline, keep the App installed until its pending-deletion message disappears after a successful connection. Local training history and progress are kept. Please do not email passwords or analytics request tokens.

Remove local data

The App keeps profiles, settings, workout history, progress, and saved game state locally on your device. Deleting the App removes its local data from the device. Data may be restored later from an Apple device or iCloud backup, depending on your backup settings.

Privacy and terms

Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

Detailed optional analytics is retained for up to 90 days. Non-identifying aggregate statistics may be kept without a time limit for long-term product and audience analysis. Turning analytics off requests deletion of linked data; previously anonymised summaries remain. See the Privacy Policy for the full retention and deletion rules.
