Support

Help with Less Phone, More Brain (formerly CleverCircuit: Brain Games)

Need help? Email sales@qiroh.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, App version, and a short description of the issue. Never send passwords, payment-card details, or Apple ID verification codes.

Restore a purchase

Open the App’s Premium screen and use Restore Purchases. Make sure your device is signed in to the Apple ID that made the purchase and has an internet connection.

Manage or cancel a subscription

On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions and select Less Phone, More Brain (previously CleverCircuit). Apple manages billing, renewal, cancellation, and trial eligibility. Deleting the App does not cancel a subscription.

Request a refund

App Store payments are processed by Apple. Refund requests can be submitted at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Remove local data

The App keeps profiles, settings, workout history, progress, and saved game state locally on your device. Deleting the App removes its local data from the device. Data may be restored later from an Apple device or iCloud backup, depending on your backup settings.
